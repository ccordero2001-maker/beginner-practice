  1. Phase 01 · Foundation

    The outlined bell

    One mark. Raw physical strength, foundational work, nothing decorative. This is where the practice starts and what everything after it is measured against.

    “Every motion begins with momentum.”

  2. Phase 02 · The swing

    Momentum, traced

    The bell takes on mass, depth and a path. The arc it burns into the screen is the energy transfer itself — load, drive, float, return.

    “Iron built the foundation. Intelligence scales the results.”

  3. Phase 03 · Impact

    Iron becomes intelligence

    At peak elevation the bell resolves into data — reps, loads, recovery, adherence — and splits into three streams: personal coaching, corporate wellness, and the app that holds both.

    “From a single bell to a complete ecosystem. Welcome to BOLD.”
