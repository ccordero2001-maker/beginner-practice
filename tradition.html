How it works

Three steps, and you'd know by week two.

No contracts to read before you understand what you're buying.

  1. 1

    We talk, then we measure

    A 45-minute conversation and a simple movement check — in person or over video. We find out what you can do now, what hurts, and what you actually want. Nothing is sold in this meeting.

    45 minutes · free · no equipment needed

  2. 2

    You get a plan with your name on it

    Written by the coach you met, not picked from a menu. It lands in the app, one day at a time, in plain language. If something in it doesn't suit you, you say so and it changes.

    Ready within 3 working days

  3. 3

    Every 12 weeks we re-test

    The same checks as day one, side by side with the first set. You see exactly what moved. If nothing did, that's our problem to fix, and we'll tell you rather than wait for you to ask.

Talk to us

Straight answers

The questions people actually ask.

I'm not fit. Is this for me?

Yes, and it's most of who we see. The first session measures where you are now — not where anyone thinks you should be. Plenty of people start unable to do a single unassisted squat, and the plan is built from that, honestly.

I'm in my sixties or seventies. Will you actually take me on?

Yes. Strength training matters more with age, not less, and the movement check exists precisely so the plan respects your joints, your history and any medication you're on. Bring your doctor's advice and we'll work inside it.

Do I have to use the app?

No. It makes things easier, and we'll sit with you once to set it up, but a printed plan and a phone call works too. Nobody gets dropped for not being technical.

We're a small business. Aren't we too small for corporate wellness?

Our smallest program is five people. Small teams are usually easier — one session a week in whatever space you have, and a one-page summary each quarter. You do not need an HR department to do this.

As an employer, do I get to see my staff's health data?

No, and that's deliberate. You see participation and totals across the group. Individual results belong to the individual. It's the part that gets programs approved and the part that keeps people signing up.

What if it isn't working?

The 12-week re-test is designed to catch that in writing. If the numbers haven't moved we say so first, and we either change the approach or help you find something that fits better. There's no lock-in on any tier.
